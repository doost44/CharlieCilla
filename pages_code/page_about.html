About The Work

Profile

I am a designer and aspiring filmmaker interested in the ways we communicate through visuals, storytelling, and subconscious cues. My practice spans filmmaking for narrative, graphic design for communication, and industrial design for functional ideas.

Approach

As a Global Business and Digital Arts student at the University of Waterloo, I explore composition, identity, and narrative across both digital and physical media. I work with Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and Premiere to combine craft with concept.
