POWERADE Rebrand

Context

A branding assignment centered on repositioning POWERADE for a more focused endurance-athlete narrative.

Problem

The brand voice felt broad and diluted, making it harder to differentiate from competitors and connect emotionally with runners.

Role

Brand Designer

Process

Defined a return-to-origin concept, then developed visual language through typography, color contrast, campaign moodboards, and pitch frames.

Outcome

Delivered a cohesive rebrand direction built around movement, resilience, and competitive momentum.

Tools

Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Presentation storytelling
