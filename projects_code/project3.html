WYAWYG? Poster Series

Context

An experimental poster series combining hand-made mark-making with digital composition.

Problem

I wanted to preserve tactile qualities of physical art while still achieving polish and structure in digital outputs.

Role

Visual Designer

Process

Created physical textures first, digitized source material, and iterated on layering, contrast, and rhythm across a five-poster set.

Outcome

Built a distinct mixed-media visual system that demonstrates style range and composition control.

Tools

Adobe Photoshop, Scanned textures, Composition studies
